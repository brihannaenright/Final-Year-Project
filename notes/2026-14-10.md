## 2026-10-14

### What I did

- luminance.py: added luminance_map(frame), a NumPy version of the WCAG luminance formula (np.where replaces if/else)
- 02_numpy_luminance.py: compared loop vs NumPy on frame 1
- 03_frame_differences.py: luminance change between consecutive frames for the whole video, plotted over time

### Results

- Tested luminance_map on a 2x2 array (blue, red, green, grey): 0.0722, 0.2126, 0.7152, 0.2159 - correct
- Loop: 120.2 ms/frame, NumPy: 7.0 ms/frame at 320x240 (~17x faster), both average 0.164027
- Frame budget at 29.97 fps = 33.4 ms - NumPy is fast enough at 320x240
- Estimated 1080p: ~190 ms/frame - too slow for real time (measure with tests)
- Whole video: 0-15 s and 41-49 s calm with single spikes, 15-41 s very busy

### Important lessons

- if/else doesn't work on arrays - use np.where
- uint8 values wrap around past 255 - convert to float before maths
- Mean of a True/False array = fraction of True values

### Problems

- Whole-frame fraction may miss a local flash (WCAG area = 25% of a 10-degree field)
- Only compares neighbouring frames - may miss changes spread over several frames
