## 2026-11-10

### What I did

- (Setup: repo, venv, folder structure)
- (01_read_video_info.py: reading a video and its properties)
- (luminance.py: relative luminance function calculation from WCAG)

### Results

- Test video: 320x240, 29.97fps, 1478 frames
- Average relative luminance of frame 1: 0.16
- Pixel-by-pixel loop: 0.13s per frame at 320x240
- Estimated 1080p: ~3.5s per frame (measure with tests)

### Important lessons

- frame.shape returns [h, w, channels]
- Python notates power as \*\* and not ^
- Averaging the pixels of each frame can miss small flashes in one portion of the frame - this will not be the final design

### Problems

- Slow loop cannot keep up with a real time capture: viewer may have been subjected to dangerous brightness before the loop detects it
